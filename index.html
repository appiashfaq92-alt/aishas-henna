Henna aftercare

A few simple steps for the darkest stain.

How long should I keep the paste on?

As long as you can, ideally 4 to 6 hours. Keep it moist with a lemon and sugar dab.

Can I wash it right away?

Scrape the paste off with a dry cloth or the back of a knife. Avoid water for the first 12 to 24 hours.

How long does the stain last?

Usually one to two weeks, depending on your skin and how often you wash your hands.

What helps the colour last?

A little oil or balm over the design, and gentle care while washing.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes. Send a photo on WhatsApp or tell us the style you like, and it will be drawn by hand to suit your hand.
